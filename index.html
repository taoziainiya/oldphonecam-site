OldPhone Cam — Support & Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 • App Store Category: Photo & Video

1. Zero-Data-Collection Privacy Guarantee

OldPhone Cam does not collect, store, transmit, track, or share any personal data, photos, location data, or device identifiers.

100% ON-DEVICE PROCESSING All camera capture and Core Image retro filter rendering happen locally on your iPhone.
ZERO ANALYTICS OR ADS No third-party tracking SDKs, no advertising identifiers (IDFA), and no cloud accounts required.
YOUR PHOTOS STAY YOURS Photos you take or import are saved solely to your iOS Photo Library when you choose to save them.

2. Privacy Policy (App Store Connect Compliance)

  • Camera Access (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used strictly in real time to display the camera viewfinder and capture photos on your device. Camera frames never leave your device.
  • Photo Library Access (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription / NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription): Used only when you explicitly save a captured photo to your Camera Roll or choose a photo via the system PhotosPicker to apply retro phone filters.
  • Data Retention & Third Parties: Because we do not operate any backend servers or collect any user data, no information is stored externally or shared with third parties.
  • Children's Privacy: Our application does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
